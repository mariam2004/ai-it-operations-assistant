# Potential security incident initial handling

General guidance only. The organization's incident-response plan and authorized responders take precedence.

## Initial safe response

If a user suspects phishing, credential theft, unusual sign-in, or malware, ask what happened and when, which device/service is involved, and whether a security alert or case ID exists. Do not ask the user to paste secrets, passwords, MFA codes, session cookies, or complete private messages into the assistant.

Do not click suspicious links, open attachments, delete logs, run unapproved cleanup commands, disable EDR/antivirus, or wipe/reimage a device before evidence is preserved. Notify the security/incident-response team using the approved reporting channel.

Only isolate a device or take disruptive action when the approved incident-response policy or an authorized responder directs it. Record actions and timestamps and share sanitized evidence through approved channels.
