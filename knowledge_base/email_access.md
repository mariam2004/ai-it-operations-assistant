# Email access and mail-flow troubleshooting

General IT guidance only; follow the organization's approved messaging runbook.

## Clarify the symptom

Determine whether the issue is sign-in, send, receive, search, attachment, or synchronization. Ask whether webmail and the desktop/mobile client behave differently, whether others are affected, when it started, and the exact sanitized error.

## Safe checks

Check the approved messaging-service status page and mailbox quota/status using authorized tools. For a non-delivery report, use its error code and message identifier; avoid sharing message body or external addresses unless policy requires them. If one client is affected, verify sync state and backups before following an approved repair procedure.

## Safety and escalation

Do not request passwords, session cookies, or full email contents. Do not delete or recreate profiles or mail data until synchronization and backup status are understood. Escalate with time, client/version, affected action, message ID, and sanitized error details.
